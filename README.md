# DETERMINATION
A habit tracker, but with a touch of Undertale.
This website is still having new updates worked onto it. Stay tuned!

Current features:
-You can rename yourself!
-A focus timer: Hatches a monster egg every 60 minutes + sparks and lowers your HP when it is stopped in the middle.
-A shop: Monster Eggs, Hats, Colours, Background Styles, Items
-A streak bar
-Trophies to gain sparks
-A cavern to keep your monsters
-Sign in to gain access to leaderboards!

Stay determined and add your suggestions!
